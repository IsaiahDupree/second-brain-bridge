Common issues

  • "Your Mac agent is offline." Start it on your Mac with second-brain-agent run and make sure the Mac is awake (tip: caffeinate -s while plugged in).
  • macOS says it can't read your vault folder. If your vault is in a protected location (Documents, Desktop, or iCloud Drive), open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Files and Folders (or Full Disk Access) and allow your terminal app to read it. This is a one-time Files-and-Folders prompt — not Automation.
  • Some notes are missing. Confirm the notes you expect are actually synced into the vault folder on your Mac (via iCloud Drive, Obsidian Sync, or your sync tool of choice) — the agent can only see files present in that folder.
  • Connector shows unauthorized. Remove and re-add the connector in your assistant to refresh the OAuth grant.
  • New notes (Pro) land in the wrong folder. Notes you create without naming a folder go to the root of your vault — name the target folder when you ask, or set a default notes folder in your agent configuration.

Uninstall / revoke access

  • Stop and unpair the Mac agent: quit the process and delete ~/.second-brain-agent.json.
  • Remove the connector in your assistant's settings.
  • Email us to delete your account and all associated data.
